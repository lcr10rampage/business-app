Pro Blade Care

Keep your edge between visits.

A sharp edge lasts a lot longer with a little care. Three habits make most of the difference.

Three Habits

Small steps, sharper tools.

01

Clean and dry before drop-off

Wipe tools down so we can inspect the true edge and give you the best result.

02

Use wood cutting boards

Wood and soft plastic protect your edge. Glass and stone boards dull a blade fast.

03

Store in blocks or magnetic racks

Loose in a drawer, blades knock together and chip. Give each one a home.

Time for a proper sharpen?

Care goes far, but every edge needs the stone eventually.
