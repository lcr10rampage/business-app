How It Works

Sharp in three simple steps.

No appointment needed for most items. Here is exactly what happens from the moment you walk in the door.

STEP 01

Drop Off

Bring your knives and tools by, clean and dry. No appointment needed for most items.

STEP 02

We Sharpen

Each blade is matched to the right stone and angle, then finished by hand.

STEP 03

Quality Check

Every edge is tested for a clean, even bite before it leaves the shop.

STEP 04

Pick Up

Most orders are ready the same week. We let you know the moment they are done.

Sound simple? It is.

Book your sharpening and drop by whenever suits you.
